1. Scope

This policy applies to VLyrics for macOS and the network services we operate specifically for VLyrics. It does not control the independent privacy practices of Apple, Spotify, lyric providers, hosting providers, or websites you visit through links in the App.

VLyrics does not currently provide user accounts, sign-in, advertising, or payment processing.

2. Information we collect and process

First-launch analytics

When VLyrics initializes for the first time, it sends a one-time report to a service operated by ariidjs Devs Team. The report is sent before onboarding is completed and contains:

  • the installed VLyrics version; and
  • the App's bundle identifier.

Playback and track information

Depending on the source you choose, VLyrics reads playback information from Apple Music, Spotify, or macOS Now Playing. This may include song title, artist, duration, playback position and state, artwork or artwork URL, shuffle state, and repeat state. This information is primarily processed locally to synchronize lyrics and player controls.

Lyrics searches and selections

To find lyrics, VLyrics sends track search information such as song title, artist name, search terms, and provider song identifiers to the selected lyric provider. Providers return lyric text and related song metadata. If you manually select a lyric result, VLyrics may remember that selection locally for future playback.

Artwork requests

Artwork from Apple Music and system Now Playing is handled locally when available. VLyrics may download artwork from a URL supplied by Spotify. If fallback artwork is needed, VLyrics may send the song title and artist to Apple's iTunes Search API and download the resulting artwork.

Remote configuration and updates

VLyrics contacts our remote configuration service to obtain compatibility, availability, and version information. VLyrics also uses Sparkle to check a GitHub Pages appcast for software updates and to download updates.

Standard network information

When VLyrics connects to us or a third-party service, the service may automatically receive your IP address, request date and time, user agent, and other standard HTTP or diagnostic information. We do not add advertising identifiers or tracking cookies to the App.

Optional audio analysis

If you enable an audio-spectrum or audio-reactive feature, VLyrics captures audio from compatible music applications and converts it into frequency magnitude data for visualization. This processing occurs locally. VLyrics does not intentionally record raw audio to disk or upload captured audio to our servers.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide synchronized lyrics and playback controls;
  • search for, rank, display, and cache lyrics;
  • display album artwork and audio-reactive visual effects;
  • remember your settings and explicitly selected lyric results;
  • measure installations and understand basic compatibility or usage patterns;
  • deliver remote configuration, service notices, and software updates;
  • diagnose errors, protect the App and services, and prevent abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, we process information as necessary to provide the App, based on your choices and permissions, for our legitimate interests in operating and improving VLyrics, and where required with your consent or to comply with law.

4. Services and disclosures

We do not sell or rent your personal information. VLyrics communicates with the following categories of service providers and third parties:

ariidjs Devs Team services First-launch analytics and remote configuration, currently hosted through Vercel infrastructure.
LRCLib Receives track title, artist, search limits, or provider song ID to locate and return lyrics.
NetEase and the NetEase search proxy Receive song search terms or provider song IDs to locate and return track metadata and lyrics.
Apple services The iTunes Search API may receive track title and artist for fallback artwork. Apple also controls macOS permissions and local Music integration.
Spotify and artwork hosts Spotify integration occurs locally through macOS Automation. Artwork URLs returned by Spotify may be downloaded from the host identified by the URL.
GitHub Pages and Sparkle Used to check for and download VLyrics software updates.

These services may process standard network information and use data according to their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or support a reorganization or transfer of the VLyrics project, subject to applicable law.

5. macOS permissions and integrations

Automation / Apple Events Allows VLyrics to read playback state and control Apple Music or Spotify. macOS manages this permission, and you can revoke it in System Settings.
System Audio Recording Used only when an audio-spectrum or audio-reactive feature needs local audio analysis. If denied, those visual effects do not move.
Now Playing / MediaRemote Reads system playback metadata locally and sends playback commands when Now Playing is your selected source.
Network access Required for lyric searches, artwork fallback, remote configuration, first-launch analytics, and software updates.

You can manage protected-resource permissions in macOS System Settings. Revoking a permission may disable the feature that depends on it but does not prevent use of unrelated VLyrics features.

6. Local storage and retention

VLyrics stores information on your Mac, including:

  • feature settings and preferences;
  • your selected player source, display, and window preferences;
  • the generated installation identifier and first-launch report status;
  • remembered lyric selections, including track title, artist, provider, provider song ID, and timestamps; and
  • cached lyric text, including synchronized or karaoke timing data.

Remembered lyric references are automatically limited to approximately 500 entries, and cached lyric text is limited to approximately 100 tracks. Older entries may be removed automatically as those limits are exceeded.

You can delete remembered lyric choices and cached lyrics through Settings > Lyrics > Clear Storage. Other preferences generally remain until you change them, reset them, or remove the App and its associated application data. First-launch analytics held by us are retained for up to 12 months. Independent providers determine retention for information they receive under their own policies.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can:

  • choose Apple Music, Spotify, or Now Playing as your playback source;
  • deny or revoke Automation and System Audio Recording permissions;
  • disable optional audio-spectrum and audio-reactive features;
  • clear locally remembered lyric choices and cached lyric text;
  • stop network lyric requests by quitting VLyrics or disconnecting network access; and
  • contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of first-launch analytics associated with you.

To help locate a first-launch record, we may need information sufficient to match your request, such as app version information, We may need to verify your request and may retain limited information where required by law, security, or recordkeeping obligations. Rights vary by location, and applicable law may provide additional rights or exceptions.

8. Security

VLyrics uses the macOS App Sandbox, system permission controls, HTTPS network connections, and signed Sparkle updates. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. You use the App and network services at your own risk.

9. Children

VLyrics is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through VLyrics, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. International processing

VLyrics and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal mechanisms for such processing or transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VLyrics changes. We will revise the "Last updated" date and may provide additional notice in the App or release notes when changes are material. Your continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.
